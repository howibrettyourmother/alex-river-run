# Alex's River Run 🛶 — a canoe adventure for Alex

A top-down canoeing game made for Alex (almost 7), inspired by a real fall day paddling the Huron River to Milford, Michigan with Dad: a dark stone arch tunnel, bridges, weeping willows, mirror-still water and yellow and orange leaves, a silver aluminum canoe, and one very lucky woolly bear caterpillar.

**Play:** https://howibrettyourmother.github.io/alex-river-run/ (landscape on iPad or iPhone; Share → Add to Home Screen gives the "River Run" icon)

- **Paddling:** big LEFT / RIGHT buttons (multi-touch). LEFT steers left and RIGHT steers right. Alternate left and right in rhythm to build POWER and speed, and hold both to brake or back-paddle. Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D, hold both (or ↓/S) to brake, Space/Enter for menus, P/Esc to pause. A "Real paddling" toggle (off by default) switches to true paddle-side steering, where paddling on the left turns you right.
- **Woolly bear rescue:** fuzzy black-and-orange caterpillars are stranded on floating leaves and sticks. Paddle close to scoop one up, then steer to the shore to drop it off. Each level has a "Woolly Bear Hero" counter, and the end screen celebrates every bear you saved.
- **Dad goes overboard** a few times per level: a goose steals his hat, he reaches for a frog, he leans too far on a sharp turn, or he does a huge sneeze. Big splash, lots of flailing and sputtering. He always lands ahead of or beside the canoe and stays on screen. The current slows down, a pulsing ring, a "Paddle to Dad!" label and an arrow show where he is, and the pickup radius is generous. If nobody reaches him in about 10 seconds, he doggy-paddles over and grabs on himself. He gets soggier each time (dripping, then a fish on his head, then a lily-pad hat).
- **Hazards:** logs, rocks, beaver dams with gaps, dive-bombing geese (a poop splat smears the screen for a moment), splashing fish, burping-bubble fish, dark stone tunnels where you only see a headlamp cone, and low bridges where everyone ducks automatically.
- **Collectibles:** golden acorns, Petoskey stones (Michigan's state stone, a fossil coral), painted turtles and frogs to wave at, and one hidden rubber duck per level.
- **Michigan touches:** a "You are here" mitten sign, great blue herons, sandhill cranes, white-tailed deer, Canada geese, painted turtles, Up North-style log cabins, fall colors, and a "Welcome to Milford" finish with cider and donuts.
- **5 levels down the Huron River:** Proud Lake (Proud Lake Recreation Area) → Huron River Marsh → Beaver Backwater → Bridges into Milford (five bridges and a dark stone tunnel) → Mill Pond Rush (make-believe fast water; the real river here is gentle). Then the boss on the Milford Mill Pond: Old Mossback, a grumpy snapping turtle (a made-up river legend). Dodge his telegraphed lunges and bring him 3 flowers until he's happy. Then it's "Welcome to Milford!" at Central Park. The real route from Proud Lake to Milford's Central Park is about 2.3 miles with five bridges (Huron River Water Trail, Heavner Canoe).
- **Unlockable canoes:** Rocket (clear Huron River Marsh), Pirate (find 3 rubber ducks), Hot Dog (make the turtle happy). Best times and a top-10 leaderboard are saved on the device.
- **First 30 seconds:** level 1 runs a short voiced tutorial (tap LEFT/RIGHT in rhythm, hold to turn, scoop the first woolly bear with a bouncing arrow, drop it ashore), with a pulsing ring on the button to press.
- **Stars (3 per level, 15 total):** All woolly bears, All Petoskey stones, No bonks. Best stars are saved per level and shown on the level select, the intro card and the title; the level-clear card explains any missed star.
- **Finish in Milford:** the end screen's title matches how you did (Woolly Bear Hero / Super Paddler / You made it to Milford) with encouraging tips, stars per level, and cider & donuts.
- **No harsh game over:** a bump costs a heart and plays a funny bonk. Run out of hearts and you get an "Oopsie!" and start again from the last checkpoint buoy.
- **Sound:** original WebAudio chiptune music for each level, synth sound effects, and warm voice lines that name Alex ("Woolly bear saved! Great job, Alex!"), played one at a time.

All art is procedural canvas and all audio is original WebAudio plus offline Piper TTS. No third-party game assets.

Dev: `src/gen.sh` (offline Piper TTS → `/tmp/arr`), `python3 src/build.py` (inlines clips and JS into `index.html`), `python3 src/icons.py` (Home Screen icons and og.png), `python3 src/test.py` (iPad 1024×768 and iPhone 844×390 landscape, portrait, touch, keyboard, stuck-input checks, control-coverage checks, and 4× CPU-throttle perf), `python3 src/fullrun.py [url] [ipad|iphone]` (scripted full playthrough of all 5 levels and the boss), `python3 src/dadtest.py` (Dad rescue with real touch input in every level, a tunnel, near the finish and the boss pond, on iPad and iPhone), `python3 src/safari.py` (in-browser mobile Safari: reduced viewport heights, rotation mid-play, no zoom, scroll or selection, audio unlock).

Input: touches are tracked per `Touch.identifier`. touchcancel, pointercancel, blur, pagehide and visibilitychange all clear input, so a paddle can never get stuck on.
